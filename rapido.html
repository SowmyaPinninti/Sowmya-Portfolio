00 — OVERVIEW

Building a premium value layer for Captains

Rapido's ride-hailing platform connects riders with Captains across a large network of cities. Its SaaS-style, zero-commission approach creates an interesting product strategy opportunity: instead of relying primarily on ride commissions, Rapido can potentially monetise additional value provided to Captains.

This case study explores a premium SaaS concept, Rapido Pro, designed around the operational needs of Captains while addressing a broader strategic monetisation challenge.

01 — CONTEXT

From Marketplace to SaaS

Traditional ride-hailing marketplaces commonly monetise transactions through commissions or platform fees. Rapido has increasingly used a subscription-oriented, zero-commission model for Captains.

This changes the product strategy equation. The platform is no longer only selling access to rides; it can also create value through tools, insights and services that help Captains operate their work more effectively.

02 — STRATEGIC ISSUE

The Monetisation Challenge

PRIMARY ISSUE

How can Rapido increase revenue per active Captain without returning to ride commissions?

Classification: Primarily a strategic product and business-model challenge.

The operational experience of Captains provides the customer insight that can inform the solution.

03 — CUSTOMER

Captains / Drivers

The primary customer for Rapido Pro is the Captain — the driver using Rapido as part of their daily work.

Rather than treating Captains only as supply on a marketplace, this concept treats them as micro-business operators who need visibility, planning and tools to manage their earnings.
